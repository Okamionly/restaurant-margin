## 💬 Maker Comment (1er commentaire)

```
Hey Hunters! 👋 Founder of RestauMargin here.

I built this because I saw too many restaurant owners spending 3-4 hours every WEEK on Excel spreadsheets, calculating food cost by hand, only to discover at month-end that their margins were 4-7 points lower than they thought.

What RestauMargin does differently:

🎙️ Voice-to-recipe in 10 seconds — Say "1 kg flour, 500g tomato, 200g mozzarella" and you get a full technical sheet with cost breakdown.

📊 Real-time margin per dish — Every recipe shows live food cost % as supplier prices change.

🔔 Supplier price drift alerts — When your tomato supplier raises prices 8%, you know immediately.

📈 Menu engineering — Auto-classify dishes as Stars / Puzzles / Plowhorses / Dogs (Boston Matrix).

🤖 AI that understands restaurant French — Built natively in French, no awkward translations.

Currently in pilot phase with French independent restaurants. Free 7-day trial, then €29/month (Pro) or €79/month (Business with multi-location).

Happy to answer any questions! What features would you want for restaurant management software?

— Youssef
```
